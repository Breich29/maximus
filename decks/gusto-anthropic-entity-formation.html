The problem

They come to Claude with a passion. Not yet a business.

Someone comes to Claude excited about a product, a craft, a service – not paperwork.

But before they can run a business, they have to officially start one: entity formation, a registered agent, EIN application, initial filing, operating agreement.

They require a real filing with real government agencies – something a conversation alone can't complete.

This is exactly where Claude can carry someone today – and exactly where it currently has to stop.

The solution

One connection. The full start your business workflow.

Introduce SYB capabilities – entity formation, RA, EIN, initial report, operating agreement – through the Gusto MCP, the same connector pattern already seeing thousands of activations from Claude users on the accountant and payroll side.

Entrepreneurs connect the Gusto MCP once and get the full formation workflow, plus a natural on-ramp into Gusto's ongoing use cases and other SMB tools customers use within Claude.
